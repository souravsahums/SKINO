TITLE
The Weight of the Adjoint: Which Symplectic Form a Neural Operator Preserves,
and Whether It Survives the Lift

AUTHORS  (arXiv wants "Forename Surname" separated by commas)
Sourav Sahu, Gareth O'Brien

ABSTRACT  (1915 characters -- arXiv's limit is 1920)
Structure-preserving neural operators are built on a question that is usually left unasked: symplectic with respect to which inner product? On a non-uniform grid the discrete L^2 adjoint is K^*=W^-1K^TW, where W collects the quadrature weights, and the choice of W silently selects which symplectic form a gradient shear preserves. We make that choice explicit, build both alternatives, and measure them. Using the symplectic defect ||WA-A^TW||_F/||WA||_F computed from the autograd Jacobian, we find a clean diagonal across 1-D, 2-D and 3-D: each construction is exact (2x10^-16) in the form its adjoint was built from and O(1) away from the other. Preserving a symplectic form is therefore cheap; the content lies in preserving the right one. On our benchmarks, all sampled on uniform grids, the variant whose form matches the grid has the lower error in 16 of 18 paired comparisons, and in all 4 when the lift and projection are removed so that the deployed map itself is symplectic, by 14-22x on three of them. The reverse direction, data on Chebyshev nodes, is untested. Over a 2x10^4-step rollout (100-200 domain transits) the matched-form lift-free operator is the only one of nine families that stays bounded in every run (12 of 12); symplectic cores inside the conventional lift-project-residual wrapper do so in at most 4, and the mismatched-form lift-free operator, though exactly symplectic, diverges in 3-D. Bounded is not accurate: at that horizon its error is comparable to the signal and its energy is off by 126-625%, against 0.03% for the reference solver. In these experiments structure preservation buys reliability rather than accuracy, and only end to end in the matched form; it is not the cost a prior version of this study reported. We also report a 783-run benchmark, and retract an earlier symplecticity theorem of ours that conflated unit Jacobian determinant with preservation of omega.
